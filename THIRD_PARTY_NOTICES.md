The packages listed in `package.json` and `package-lock.json` remain subject to
their authors' licenses. Installing or building Contexta does not relicense
those packages under the Contexta source-available notice. Their package
metadata and license files are the authoritative source for their terms.

## Music attribution record

This public repository contains no background-music binaries or playback code.
The attribution record in `src/assets/music/LICENSE.md` documents music used by
a separately maintained private version of Contexta. Those tracks remain
subject to the Pixabay Content License and are not included in the Contexta
license grant.
