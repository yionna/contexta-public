# Third-party notices

Contexta combines original project material with third-party software. The
repository-level `LICENSE.md` applies only to material for which the
Contexta copyright holder has the right to grant those terms.

## Software dependencies

The packages listed in `package.json` and `package-lock.json` remain subject to
their authors' licenses. Installing or building Contexta does not relicense
those packages under the Contexta source-available notice. Their package
metadata and license files are the authoritative source for their terms.

## Project artwork

The Contexta favicon and cat artwork are project-specific assets. Their known
provenance is documented in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`; they are not covered by any
third-party software dependency license.
